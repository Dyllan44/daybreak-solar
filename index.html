Planning your installation

How do I know which system is right for my home?

Start with your electricity use, roof space and priorities. An assessment can help you compare solar, storage or a combination for your property.

What’s involved in the installation process?

Planning usually starts with a site assessment and a system design. The installer can then explain the scope, expected timing and checks for your property.

Can I add battery backup later?

It depends on the existing equipment and what your home needs. If storage is a future goal, raise it when comparing inverter and system options.
